### Decision-making by directors

- [Article 12 – Directors to take decisions collectively](/articles/article-12.md): Directors' decisions must be majority decisions at a meeting or unanimous decisions under article 13, unless the company has only one director.
- [Article 13 – Unanimous decisions](/articles/article-13.md): A unanimous decision is taken when all eligible directors indicate that they share a common view, provided they would have formed a quorum.
- [Article 14 – Calling a directors’ meeting](/articles/article-14.md): Any director may call a directors' meeting by giving every director notice of the date, time, place and how remote participants will communicate.
- [Article 15 – Participation in directors’ meetings](/articles/article-15.md): Directors take part in a meeting when it has been properly called and they can all communicate with each other, wherever they are.
- [Article 16 – Quorum for directors’ meetings](/articles/article-16.md): The quorum for directors' meetings is two unless the directors fix a higher number, and it can never be fewer than two.
- [Article 17 – Chairing of directors’ meetings](/articles/article-17.md): The directors may appoint and remove a chairman, and must choose a stand-in if the chairman has not joined within ten minutes.
- [Article 18 – Casting vote](/articles/article-18.md): The chairman has a casting vote when votes are tied, unless the chairman is not counted for quorum or voting purposes.
- [Article 19 – Conflicts of interest](/articles/article-19.md): A director with an interest in a transaction with the company is not counted for quorum or voting except in permitted cases; the chairman rules on disputes.
- [Article 20 – Records of decisions to be kept](/articles/article-20.md): The company must keep a written record of every unanimous or majority decision of the directors for at least ten years.
- [Article 21 – Directors’ discretion to make further rules](/articles/article-21.md): The directors may make further rules about how they take and record decisions.
