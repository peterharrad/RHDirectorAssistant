# Definitions

(1) In the articles, unless the context requires otherwise:

## articles

“articles” means the company’s articles of association;

## bankruptcy

“bankruptcy” includes individual insolvency proceedings in a jurisdiction other than England and Wales or Northern Ireland which have an effect similar to that of bankruptcy;

## chairman

“chairman” has the meaning given in [article 17](/articles/article-17.md);

## chairman of the meeting

“chairman of the meeting” has the meaning given in [article 30](/articles/article-30.md);

## Companies Acts

“Companies Acts” means the Companies Acts as defined in section 2 of the Companies Act 2006(5), in so far as they apply to the company;

## director

“director” means a director of the company, and includes any person occupying the position of director, by whatever name called;

## document

“document” includes, unless otherwise specified, any document sent or supplied in electronic form;

## electronic form

“electronic form” has the meaning given in section 1168 of the Companies Act 2006 (“the 2006 Act”):

## immediate landlord

“immediate landlord” in relation to a unit in the Premises, means the person who:

(a) if the unit is subject to a lease, is the landlord under the lease, or

(b) if the unit is subject to two or more leases, is the landlord under whichever of the leases is inferior to the others;

## Lease

“Lease” means a long lease within the meaning of sections 76 and 77 of the 2002 Act;

## member

“member” has the meaning given in section 112 of the 2006 Act;

## ordinary resolution

“ordinary resolution” has the meaning given in section 282 of the 2006 Act;

## participate

“participate”, in relation to a directors’ meeting, has the meaning given in [article 15](/articles/article-15.md);

## the Premises

“the Premises” means; Riverside House, Fobney Street, READING. The flats within Riverside House are addressed on their registered titles as either RG1 6BH or RG1 6BJ, both postcodes referring to the same premises.

## proxy notice

“proxy notice” has the meaning given in [article 36](/articles/article-36.md);

## residential unit

“residential unit” means a flat or any other separate set of premises which is constructed or adapted for use for the purposes of a dwelling;

## RTM company

“RTM company” (Right to Manage Company) has the meaning given in section 73 of the Commonhold and Leasehold Reform Act 2002;

## special resolution

“special resolution” has the meaning given in section 283 of the 2006 Act;

## subsidiary

“subsidiary” has the meaning given in section 1159 of the 2006 Act;

## writing

“writing” means the representation or reproduction of words, symbols or other information in a visible form by any method or combination of methods, whether sent or supplied in electronic form or otherwise; and

## 2002 Act

“2002 Act” means the Commonhold and Leasehold Reform Act 2002.
