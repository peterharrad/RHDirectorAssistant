# Riverside House Management Lease

- [Definitions](/leases/management-lease/definitions.md): Terms used in the management lease, most of them taking their meaning from the standard form of flat lease.
- [Clause 2 – Interpretations](/leases/management-lease/clause-02.md): How the lease is to be read: gender and number, persons, references and headings, statutes, negative covenants, and joint and several liability.
- [Clause 3 – Demise](/leases/management-lease/clause-03.md): The lessor grants the manager a 999-year lease of the common parts and grounds, with the rights in the First Schedule and subject to those in the Second, the ground rents staying with the lessor.
- [Clause 4 – The Managers Covenants](/leases/management-lease/clause-04.md): The manager's obligations: sharing maintenance accounts with the lessor, using the common parts only as such, allowing inspection, not building, altering or assigning without consent, meeting section 146 costs, and handing back in repair.
- [Clause 5 – Agreements and Declarations](/leases/management-lease/clause-05.md): An 80-year perpetuity period, the lessor joining the manager in action against flat lessees, the lessor's right of re-entry after 28 days' notice of a breach, and no third-party rights.
- [Clause 6 – The Lessors Covenants with the Manager](/leases/management-lease/clause-06.md): The manager's quiet enjoyment, and the lessor's promise to take on the manager's obligations if the manager goes into liquidation or fails to perform them.
- [First Schedule](/leases/management-lease/schedule-first.md): Rights granted to the manager: running services through the flats, support and protection, communal telecoms apparatus, and passage over the estate roads and footpaths.
- [Second Schedule](/leases/management-lease/schedule-second.md): Rights reserved to the lessor and flat lessees: running services through the common parts, access to repair the flats, and support and shelter.
